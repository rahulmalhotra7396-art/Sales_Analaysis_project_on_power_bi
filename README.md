# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** developed using **Power BI** to analyze sales, profit, customer distribution, product performance, and order trends across different years and regions.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, maps, slicers, and trend analysis, enabling stakeholders to make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to:

- Monitor overall business performance.
- Identify top-performing products and product categories.
- Analyze sales and profit trends.
- Understand customer distribution across regions.
- Evaluate yearly sales performance.
- Support strategic business decision-making through interactive reporting.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

- **Power BI Desktop**
- **Power Query**
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**
- **Data Modeling**
- **Microsoft Bing Maps**
- **Excel / CSV Dataset**

# 🔍 Business Insights

## 1️⃣ Product Performance Analysis

### Top-Selling Products

The dashboard identifies the highest revenue-generating products.

#### Top Product

🚗 **1992 Ferrari 360 Spider Red**

Generated approximately **$276.84K** in sales, making it the highest-selling product in the dataset.

Other top-performing products include:

- 2001 Ferrari Enzo
- 1952 Alpine Renault
- 2003 Harley-Davidson
- 1968 Ford Mustang

### Key Insight

A small group of premium vehicle models contributes significantly to total revenue.

---

## 2️⃣ Product Line Analysis

The pie chart shows the contribution of each product line to overall sales.

### Product Line Contribution

| Product Line | Sales Contribution |

Sales were analyzed across multiple years to identify growth patterns.
